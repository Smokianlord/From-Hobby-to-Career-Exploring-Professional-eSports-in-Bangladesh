# From Hobby to Career: Exploring Professional eSports in Bangladesh

A university research project at BRAC University on why young people in Bangladesh choose eSports as a career, and what stands in their way: family and social views, money, and balancing it with studies.

**Authors:** MD Rifat Rahman, MD Showrav Zaman, Jannatun Noor (BRAC University).

## What we did

- **Mixed methods:** an online survey plus in-person and online interviews.
- **Participants:** 86 people from the Bangladeshi eSports scene, from casual players to professionals and organisers: 64 survey responses and 22 interviews, found through snowball sampling.
- **Analysis:** grouped the answers into themes and wrote them up as an ACM-format paper and a conference poster.

## Main findings

- Passion for gaming is the main reason players go professional.
- Many struggle to convince their families and to balance eSports with study and personal life.
- Income is still unstable, but the scene is growing and creating new kinds of jobs.
- Players say government support and recognition are needed for it to keep growing.
